HOW TO READ THE LABELS
Four statuses.
Different questions.
The original dossiers carry these labels. They identify the kind of assertion being made; they are not a score for the usefulness of a subject.
Established
Described by the archive as mainstream science or scholarship. Inspect the cited source and the scope of the claim.
Historical
A documented text, event or recorded proposal. Historical existence does not establish physical performance.
Fringe
A claim or source reported without endorsement. Its provenance and testable predictions remain separate questions.
Speculative
An interpretation, hypothesis or proposed design. Missing measurements and unresolved alternatives stay visible.
