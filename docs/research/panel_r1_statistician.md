# ROUND 1 — Dr. D. (Statistician, experimental design / randomization inference)

## Position (≤200 words)

The v1 proposal gestures at the right machinery (randomized schedule, OSF preregistration,
gated unblinding, "publish either way") but is statistically hollow: **no estimand, no
SESOI, no power calculation, and an unblinding gate ("≥3 pairs") that is mathematically
incapable of supporting any inference.** With n = 3 position pairs, the finest two-sided
permutation p-value achievable is 2/2³ = 0.25 — the gate can never reject anything, so it
protects nothing. Worse, [dim07] shows the one-reference topology makes slow drift
*identically equal* to the estimand unless roles are swapped or a third device is added;
no amount of randomization fixes a confounded design. The 230 kHz band conflict
[dim04, dim05] matters to me only insofar as the band sets the systematic floors (37.6 Hz/K
[dim02]) that the SESOI must respect. I can support an amended protocol: paired
randomization-test inference on a drift-robust estimand, swap/dither symmetry, sham
injections, a pilot-for-variance stage, and a preregistered equivalence/upper-limit null.
Absent the swap/third-device fix, my vote is **do not run** — the experiment would be an
unblinded drift measurement wearing a blindfold.

## Fatal flaws (numbered, with magnitudes)

1. **No estimand defined.** "Shifts its resonant frequency beyond ordinary systematics" is
   not an estimand. Required: Δf = f_moved(x_B) − f_moved(x_A), defined per randomized
   assignment, conditional on a preregistered drift model, measured against the reference
   sphere *and* a stability witness. Without this, "effect" and "aging drift" [dim07] share
   one parameter and are non-identifiable.
2. **≥3-pairs reveal gate is vacuous.** Paired permutation/sign machinery: minimum
   achievable two-sided p with n pairs is 2/2ⁿ. n=3 → p ≥ 0.25; n=10 → p ≥ 0.002. A gate
   that cannot distinguish signal from noise at any α < 0.25 is not a gate; it is a
   superstition. Minimum defensible: n ≥ 20 pairs for permutation granularity ~2e-6, plus
   effect-size gate, not just p.
3. **Confounded design (drift ≡ signal).** [dim07]: matched-pair thermal tracking 92–97%,
   per-device aging non-common-mode, one-reference = "singular problem." Over a multi-day
   schedule, a linear drift of even 0.1 Hz/day aliases into any position-correlated
   assignment pattern. Randomization protects against *unobserved* confounding on average
   but not against a deterministic drift correlated with schedule run-length. Fix is
   structural (swap or 3rd device), not analytical.
4. **No SESOI / no power analysis.** Error budget spans 1 Hz – 2 kHz depending on mount
   [dim05]. If per-pair SD σ ≈ 2 kHz (non-kinematic remount), then even n=1000 pairs gives
   MDE(80%) ≈ 2.8σ/√n ≈ 178 Hz — the experiment would be dead on arrival and nobody would
   know. SESOI must be set from the systematic floor *after* mount engineering, and n
   derived from it.
5. **Static A/B contrasts invite drift aliasing.** [dim06] Dayton Miller lesson:
   position-periodic fake signals from thermal drift. Static A/B with slow cadence is the
   exact failure mode. Modulation (dither + lock-in) is required to move the estimand away
   from DC drift.
6. **No sequential/Type-I policy stated.** "Gated unblinding" with multiple looks and no
   alpha-spending plan inflates Type I error; with a long-shot hypothesis, a nominal 5%
   peeking-inflated error rate is how null experiments produce publications.

## Concrete amendments (numbered, actionable)

1. **Estimand (preregister verbatim):** Δf = mean over B-assigned dwells of lock-in
   demodulated (f_moved − f_ref) minus same over A-assigned dwells, at modulation
   frequency f_mod, after regression on preregistered covariates (chamber T at both
   positions, barometric P, humidity). Secondary estimand: same with sphere roles swapped.
   Inference: paired randomization (permutation) test over the actual assignment sequence,
   two-sided α = 0.01, plus 95% inversion interval → the null result is reported as an
   **upper limit |Δf| < U** and an **equivalence statement** if U < SESOI (TOST logic).
2. **Break the drift degeneracy:** (a) add a third stationary sphere as drift witness, or
   (b) swap mover/reference roles every block of 4 pairs. Either converts drift from a bias
   into a variance component. Cite [dim07]; non-negotiable.
3. **Modulate, don't step:** dither stage, position period 10–100 s, lock-in at f_mod with
   bandwidth ≪ drift spectrum; retain ≥4 slow A/B dwells per day only as a cross-check.
   [dim06]
4. **Schedule design:** block-randomized assignments (blocks of 4, balance-enforced,
   |running imbalance| ≤ 2), schedule generated by randomizr [dim03], SHA-256 hash
   deposited with third-party custody before first data [dim09]. Interleave **sham moves**
   (schedule says "move," stage does not) at ≥10% of trials and **zero-signal injections**
   (synthetic Δf added in software to a preregistered subset) at ≥5% — pipeline must
   recover injected offsets within ±10% or the unblinding gate stays shut [dim06].
5. **Pilot-for-variance:** 20 blinded pairs used *only* to estimate σ_pair and drift
   spectrum (no effect estimate unblinded); then fix n from MDE = 2.8·σ_pilot/√n ≤ SESOI/3
   at 80% power, α = 0.01. Example: if SESOI = 3 Hz and post-mount σ_pair = 1 Hz, n ≈ 31
   pairs; if σ_pair = 10 Hz, n ≈ 3100 pairs — in which case we renegotiate SESOI or the
   mount, not the statistics.
6. **SESOI definition:** SESOI = max(3 × combined systematic floor after engineering
   [dim05: thermal 1–14 Hz, remount ≤2 Hz *only if* kinematic/nodal mount, barometric
   ±0.3 Hz], or a physically motivated bound stated in the preregistration). Honest
   expectation: SESOI ~5–15 Hz @ 230 kHz (Δf/f ~ 2–7e-5). This is ~14 orders of magnitude
   weaker than cavity prior art [dim10] — the preregistration must say so explicitly so the
   null result is not oversold.
7. **Unblinding gates (staged, sequential):** Gate 1: pipeline frozen + hash-verified
   analysis code (executable plan [dim09]). Gate 2: sham/injection recovery passed. Gate 3:
   drift-model diagnostics (residual whiteness, Allan deviation plateau consistent with
   noise model) on blinded data. Gate 4: n complete. Only then: reveal labels, run the
   single preregistered test. Any interim look uses O'Brien–Fleming-style alpha spending,
   declared in advance; maximum 2 looks.
8. **Deviation policy:** bounded [dim09]: any deviation logged with timestamp + reason
   within 24 h, hashed; deviations affecting >5% of pairs trigger sensitivity re-analysis
   under worst-case inclusion/exclusion; post-unblinding changes to the analysis are
   labeled "exploratory" and cannot alter the primary p-value.
9. **Multiplicity:** one primary endpoint (Δf at f_mod). All other frequencies/modes are
   exploratory with BH-FDR or are simply reported descriptively.

## What I want to hear from the other panelists

- **Physicist/metrologist:** a defensible per-pair σ budget *after* the kinematic-mount and
  thermal fixes — is σ_pair ≤ 1 Hz at the corrected breathing-mode frequency realistic, or
  are we at 10–100 Hz? My n depends entirely on this number.
- **Instrumentation:** confirmation that a dither stage at 10–100 s period + lock-in does
  not inject its own Δf (microphonics, cable stress) above 0.1 Hz; and whether a third
  sphere fits the chamber.
- **Whoever owns the 230 kHz claim:** the corrected target frequency [dim04] — SESOI and
  thermal floor scale linearly with f (37.6 Hz/K at 230 kHz; ~1.7 Hz/K at 10.5 kHz), so
  the *correct* frequency may be statistically far cheaper to bound tightly.
- **All:** agreement that the ≥3-pairs gate is replaced by the staged gate above; if anyone
  defends n=3, I want to see the permutation distribution they have in mind.
