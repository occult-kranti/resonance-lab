# ROUND 1 — Dr. E. (Methodologist / Skeptic)

## Position (≤200 words)

LOC-1's *design ethos* is unusually good for a fringe-claim test — blinding, preregistration, publish-either-way [dim09]. But the experiment as specified is a well-instrumented test of a **physically misidentified target**: 230 kHz cannot be the breathing mode of a 10 cm Cu sphere; theory and GW-sphere data put it at 10.5–21 kHz, and 230 kHz implies R≈0.9 cm [dim04, dim05]. This is the classic pathological-science opener: the anomaly is defined loosely enough that any null can be deflected ("wrong mode, wrong Q, wrong coupling"). The one-reference topology makes drift indistinguishable from signal [dim07] — the exact structure that produced Dayton Miller's fake ether drift [dim06]. And the thermal budget (1–14 Hz gradients, up to 2 kHz remount stress) sits 3–5 orders of magnitude above the mHz instrument floor [dim05, dim02], so the experiment is systematic-limited, not sensitivity-limited. The channeled provenance is not disqualifying — hypotheses may come from anywhere — but it raises the required standard of firewalling: the source must have **zero authority over analysis, interpretation, or post-hoc re-specification**. My verdict: salvageable, but only if the claim is pinned down in writing, with numbers, BEFORE data-taking. Otherwise we are manufacturing an unfalsifiable.

## Fatal flaws

1. **Unpinned claim = unfalsifiable.** No effect size, no sign, no scaling law is specified anywhere in v1. Without a committed magnitude, every null is answerable by "smaller than sensitivity" — the water-memory failure mode (Benveniste's effect always shrank to match the noise floor). Fatal unless amended.
2. **Wrong-mode hazard.** The claimed 230 kHz band is physically incompatible with the stated hardware [dim04, dim05]. A null at 230 kHz tests nothing the proponent can be held to; a "positive" at 230 kHz on a 10 cm sphere would more likely indicate a spurious resonance (cable, mount, EM pickup) than a breathing mode. Either outcome is uninterpretable as designed.
3. **Drift ≡ signal topology.** One stationary reference + one moved device, static A/B scheduling: differential aging is non-common-mode and matched-pair tracking is only 92–97% [dim07]. This is precisely Miller 1925/1933: slow drift aliased into a position-periodic "signal" by the schedule [dim06]. A positive result from this geometry would be evidence of nothing.
4. **Systematics dominate by ~3 orders of magnitude.** Thermal gradients alone: 1–14 Hz; remount: up to 2 kHz [dim05]. Copper's −37.6 Hz/K @230 kHz means a 1 mHz resolution demands ≲27 µK control [dim02]. v1 specifies no thermal performance number at all. An effect claim must clear the systematics envelope with ≥100× margin, not the instrument floor.
5. **Q mismatch signals unanchored design.** Assumed Q≈18k vs literature ~1e5 for vacuum Cu at RT [dim04]. When the design's basic parameters disagree with measurement by 5×, the error budget built on them is fiction.
6. **Provenance firewall absent.** Channeled-source claims historically come with an advocate who reinterprets results. v1 contains no rule preventing post-hoc consultation of the source to rescue a null (goalpost moving) or to declare victory on noise (cold fusion's "excess heat" at the calorimetry noise floor).

## Concrete amendments

1. **Pre-committed claim sheet (before any hardware finalization).** Written, dated, hashed statement from the source/proponent specifying: which mode, expected frequency (to within factor 2), expected shift magnitude (Hz), sign, and what observation would count as *disconfirmation*. No magnitude → define SESOI by sensitivity analysis [dim09] and a "credibility cliff": null below X Hz = claim rejected, no retuning permitted. File on OSF with timestamp.
2. **Stage-0 mode-identification gate.** Before any position test: LDV mode-map both spheres, identify the actual breathing mode (~10–21 kHz predicted [dim04, dim05]), measure Q (expect ~1e5), and require the proponent to re-commit in writing to the *measured* mode as the test target. If 230 kHz is insisted upon, document that the geometry is then R≈0.9 cm and the current spheres are the wrong apparatus.
3. **Kill static A/B.** Replace with continuous dither: motorize the sphere on a slow rotary/reciprocating stage (e.g., ±0.20 m sinusoid at ~0.01–0.1 Hz) and lock-in detect at the dither frequency and harmonics [dim06]. Add a third sphere or weekly swap of roles [dim07]. Direction-reversal runs and label-scramble nulls mandatory [dim06].
4. **g-2-style hidden offset.** A trusted third party injects a secret, logged frequency offset into the analysis pipeline; unblinding is gated and ceremonially logged [dim06, dim09]. No analyst sees absolute frequencies until sign-off.
5. **Sham injections (Big Dog protocol).** Zero-signal and known-fake-signal runs interleaved blind: schedule executed, nothing moved; synthetic shifts injected at the clock/reference level. The analysis pipeline must (a) detect injected fakes, (b) report nulls on shams. Pipeline is frozen only after passing both [dim06].
6. **Systematics coupling matrix, published.** Exaggerate each disturbance 10–100× (heat one sphere by 4 K, tilt by 10× spec, vary pressure 10×) and measure couplings before data-taking [dim06]. The final error budget must use *measured* couplings, not estimates, and demonstrate ≥100× rejection of the SESOI.
7. **Thermal performance contract.** Written spec: ≤30 µK differential over the 0.4 m baseline for mHz claims [dim02], verified by a 2-week empty-chamber drift run analyzed blind. No data-taking until the chamber passes its own spec.
8. **Governance and disclosure rules (signed before data-taking):**
   - Hashed randomization schedule held by an independent custodian; executable analysis code frozen and containerized [dim09, dim03].
   - Raw data immutable, timestamped, auto-archived; **no deletions, no quality grading applied before the blind analysis is locked** (quality grading post-hoc is how N-ray and water-memory labs selected their way to positives — all exclusion criteria must be preregistered with quantitative thresholds).
   - Bounded-deviation policy: any protocol change triggers a documented amendment + re-blinding; unbounded exploration explicitly labeled exploratory and barred from the confirmatory claim [dim09].
   - **No public/press statements before independent replication** (the cold fusion rule: press conference before peer review destroyed the field's credibility). Positive internal result triggers a pre-agreed replication request to an independent lab with their own hardware; until then, language is "unconfirmed anomaly."
   - Equivalence-bound null reporting with the preregistered SESOI [dim09]; a null is a publishable upper bound, not a failure.
   - Provenance disclosure: the paper states plainly that the hypothesis originated from a channeled source. This is honest, costs nothing, and inoculates against later "hidden motive" criticism.
9. **Source-firewall clause.** The proponent/source may propose, in writing, before unblinding; after unblinding, source input is barred from the confirmatory analysis. Any post-hoc reinterpretation (different mode, different magnitude, "wrong phase of moon") is logged publicly as a *new* hypothesis requiring a *new* preregistration.

## What I want to hear from the other panelists

- **From the physicist:** one number — is there *any* standard-physics mechanism (or even the proponent's stated mechanism) predicting a position-dependent Δf at the 0.4 m scale above nHz, and which mode should we actually interrogate? [dim10 says all prior position-dependence tests are null to 1e-19.]
- **From the instrumentalist/engineer:** a committed, testable thermal/mechanical performance spec (µK, nm of mount repeatability) and measured CMRR for the differential readout — not estimates. And: can a dither stage hold the sphere at the required stability while moving?
- **From the statistician:** the SESOI derivation, the equivalence-bound procedure, and the exact blinding/unblinding state machine — who holds keys, when gates open.
- **From whoever represents the claim's provenance:** will they sign the pre-committed claim sheet (Amendment 1) — including what disconfirms? If they will not, I move that we say so on the record: an experiment whose advocate refuses falsification conditions is not an experiment, it is a demonstration.
