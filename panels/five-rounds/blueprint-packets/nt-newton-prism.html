Technical terms

Not to scale

A sketch may show relationships without preserving size ratios.

Use written dimensions and declared coordinate units rather than measuring screen pixels.

Units: drawing convention; not a measurement

  • An accurate-looking diagram is not an engineering drawing or a calibrated photograph.

Refractive index

Refractive index describes light propagation relative to vacuum.

The prism drawing uses stipulated indices, not a measured wavelength-dependent glass model.

Units: dimensionless

  • n here differs from integer acoustic mode indices.

Wavelength and angle of incidence

Wavelength is the spatial length of a wave cycle.

Incidence/refraction angles in Snell’s law are measured from the surface normal; a drawing direction needs its own axis reference.

Units: metre (m), often nanometre (nm); rad or declared degrees

  • Do not confuse a 20° drawing direction with a surface-normal incidence without checking geometry.

SI and unit prefixes

SI is the international system of measurement units.

cm=0.01 m, mm=0.001 m, µ=10⁻⁶, milli=10⁻³ and nano=10⁻⁹ of the stated unit.

Units: declared physical unit

  • A source drawing in cm or mm is not silently a metres-based parts table.

Reading handbook

Newton-style prism control study

A dimensioned modern first-prism illustration with computed Snell-law ray paths for three stipulated indices.

Goal: Understand how a declared prism geometry and chosen refractive indices redirect rays, while keeping a modern calculation separate from Newton’s full historical inference.

Follow the signal

  1. An aperture restricts the illustrated incident ray bundle.
  2. The stipulated refractive index and interface angle determine the calculated ray direction.
  3. A screen records a calculated ray location for each selected wavelength.
  4. A second-prism and incidence control would be separate steps in a future physical study.

What the source defines

  • This record is a source-supported modern reconstruction, not a performed optical experiment.
  • Its geometry uses explicitly stated millimetres and angles; those units must not be silently treated as metres.
  • Original source status: source_supported_modern_reconstruction.

What remains unknown

  • Actual glass dispersion, intensity, diffraction and the finite solar disk are not modeled.
  • No real prism material, calibration, uncertainty or repeatability has been verified by this illustration.

Measurement checklist

  • For a physical study, record prism/aperture geometry, incidence, screen distance and wavelength calibration.
  • Keep geometric lengths and angle conventions explicit; record repeatability rather than assuming perfect rays.
  • For an actual study, record prism angle, aperture width, incidence, screen distance, wavelength calibration and repeatability.
  • Add a second-prism discrimination and aperture/incidence controls before interpreting the full historical inference.

Questions before a physical experiment

  • Which refractive index is stipulated for which wavelength?
  • Which conclusion requires a second-prism control rather than this one drawing?
  • Is a number an angle in degrees or radians, or a length in millimetres?
